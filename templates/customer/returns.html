Policy Guidelines

1
Check Product at Time of Delivery

Customers can check the product right at the time of delivery with the courier rider. If there is any visible issue or something is wrong with the product, it can be checked immediately upon receipt.

2
Within 1 Day: Device Compatibility Exchange

If within 1 day (24 hours) of receiving your order you find that the product is not compatible with your device (e.g. phone, laptop, or system), you can deliver it back to us and exchange it with any other product from our store catalog.

Conditions for Exchange:
  • The product's packaging and box must NOT be damaged or torn.
  • The product must be completely scratchless, pristine, and unused.
3
Careful Unboxing Recommendation
Important Tip: It is strongly recommended for the user to unbox with careful hands so that in case you want to claim an exchange, the box and packaging remain in original, undamaged condition.
4
Warehouse Drop-Off & Return Location

For exchanges, customers can deliver the product back to our warehouse located at: {{ site_settings.get('store_address', 'Gulistan-e-Jauhar, Karachi, Pakistan') }}

5
Faulty Products & Warranty Claims

In case of any faulty product or manufacturing defect, customers can claim official warranty according to the product's designated warranty duration by delivering it to our {{ site_settings.get('returns_warehouse_location', 'Gulistan-e-Jauhar, Karachi') }} location.
